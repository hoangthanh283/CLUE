## Novelty evidence (top 5)
- **#3 masked replay CE**: no prior hit for label-space-masked replay cross-entropy on a growing token-classification head (queries: "replay buffer stale labels masked cross entropy class-incremental NER", "label space mask replay logits class incremental"). Nearest: IS3 (ACL 2024) scales old-row gradients on *live* data; CPFD pseudo-labels *live* O tokens; neither touches the buffer's loss. Related in spirit to CIL logit-masking tricks (e.g. "masked softmax" in SS-IL / ER-ACE for image CIL) — cite ER-ACE (Caccia et al., ICLR 2022) as the closest ancestor; the document/token-level and replay-buffer application is new.
- **#1 depth-targeted replay gradients**: nearest "Mitigating Forgetting in CL with Selective Gradient Projection" (arXiv 2603.26671) and TAPGP (ICCV 2025) — projection, not depth-gating of the replay loss by a measured locus. No direct hit.
- **#16 head-row gradient-conflict diagnostic**: "Revisiting Replay and Gradient Alignment for Continual Pre-training" (arXiv 2508.01908) studies alignment globally; per-head-row conflict on a growing BIO head not found.
- **#9 two-phase**: generic "learn-then-consolidate" schedules exist (sleep/wake-style, 2020–23); not novel.
- **#2 agreement gating**: covered by 2508.01908 and GEM/A-GEM lineage → novelty 1.

## Notes
- #3 and #16 form one package (diagnosis → fix) and are label-free, so they are the optimisation-lens complement to the parent's CoLaR-R (label refresh): if both lift CIL-CORD, the paper can say *either* fix the labels or mask the loss; if only CoLaR-R works, the new-class information (not just the stale gradient) is what matters.
- Scope overlap flagged: #3 touches the replay loss, not the replay content; left in this lens as a gradient mechanism.
- Everything here is backbone-agnostic and fits bs 1–2 on 6 GB; #3/#1/#16 are each < 40 LOC in `latent_replay.py` / `er.py`.
