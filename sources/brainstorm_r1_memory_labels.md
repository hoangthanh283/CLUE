## Novelty lookups (WebSearch extended, 2024–26)

- #1 label union on re-encountered documents: no direct hit. Closest: RCLPOD — "Replay Consolidation with Label Propagation for Continual Object Detection" (arXiv 2409.05650) propagates *predicted* labels onto replay samples; continual-NER work (CPFD 2023, Stability-Plasticity 2508.03259, GenCNER 2510.11444, Type-Balanced 2608.31038) treats the O-shift with pseudo-labels/distillation and assumes disjoint samples. Exact-label union via document identity is unreported. N=2 kept.
- #2 sliced softmax: known in CIL as separated/masked softmax (SS-IL; "Revisiting Softmax Masking" arXiv 2309.14808); not reported on latent token-level replay. N=1.
- #3 abstention on memory: CPFD masks uncertain O on live data; RCLPOD on replay predictions; mask-on-memory for latent replay not found. N=2.
- #4 inversion audit of a latent replay store: activation/model inversion exists (DRAG ICML'25; activation-inversion ACL'25; survey arXiv 2501.18934); not applied to latent replay memories as a privacy audit. N=2.
- #5 layout-consistent pseudo labels: PLBR (TKDE 2024) and CRMSP (Neurocomputing 2024) do semi-supervised KIE pseudo-labeling; BIO/line propagation for a CL memory not found. N=2 → kept at 2 but S low.

## Notes
- #1 and #2 are also the two controls the CoLaR-R programme needs to be interpretable (exact-vs-pseudo labels; positive labels vs removed negatives). Run both regardless of which "wins".
- #4 is the one memory-lens idea that adds a vision/privacy figure a CVPR reviewer will ask for ("you store activations — are they private?").
