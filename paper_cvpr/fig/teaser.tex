\captionof{figure}{\textbf{Two causes of forgetting, and a remedy matched to each.} (a) Class-incremental CORD, LayoutLMv3, AA under session-restricted scoring; bars are the cell means of \cref{tab:twobytwo}, which gives the seeds and protocol of each cell. A whole-document memory alone and a session-blocked readout alone both stay far below joint training; together they close most of the gap to it, and one low-rank delta per session with a blocked readout (SBR-Recall) comes within $\nRecallJointGap$ points of it on every seed without a memory, while a single low-rank adapter shared by all sessions with a shared softmax (our CL-LoRA port) collapses. (b) Layers $1..k$ are frozen after the first session; the memory stores per-document SVDs of layer-$k$ activations and re-grounds the drifting upper layers (CoLaR); each session gets its own background logit and tokens are routed with no task identity (SBR); SBR-Recall evaluates each block under the trunk delta of the session that created it.}
